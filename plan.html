Spot Details

📍 All Spots at a Glance

  • Ganpatipule Mandir — Open 5 AM–9 PM. Free. 1 km walk from Bhakta Niwas. Swayambhu (self-emerged) Ganesha idol.
  • Arey Vare Beach — 14 km south. Open 24 hrs. Free entry. Activities: zip line ₹150, camel ride, bullock cart.
  • Bhandarpule Viewpoint — 3–5 km from Arey Vare. Free. Hilltop view of Konkan coast.
  • Jai Vinayak Mandir, Jaigad — 19 km north. Free. Built by JSW. Art gallery inside.
  • Jaigad Fort — 2 km from Jai Vinayak. Free entry. 9 AM–6 PM. 16th century coastal fort.
  • Jaigad Lighthouse — Right next to fort. ₹20 entry. Open ONLY 4–5 PM daily. Don't be late.
Tips

🌊 Things to Know

  • Hot water at Bhakta Niwas only in the morning — shower on arrival.
  • Fill petrol before the Day 2 dawn ride — petrol pumps won't be open at 5:30 AM.
  • Arey Vare water is calm enough to swim — enjoy it in the afternoon on Day 1.
  • Carry cash — most spots near Ganpatipule and Jaigad don't accept UPI reliably.
  • Sunday bus is your ride home — missing it means stuck till Monday morning buses. Don't miss it.
  • Konkani thali at beach shacks is better and cheaper than any restaurant near the temple.
  • Weather end of March: warm, some humidity. Light clothes, chappal + shoes both needed.
🔦
Lighthouse reminder — most critical timing of the trip Jaigad Lighthouse opens ONLY 4:00–5:00 PM. If you arrive at 5:01 PM it is closed. The entire Sunday plan is structured so you finish lunch by 2:15 PM and are waiting at the lighthouse by 3:55 PM. Don't let lunch or the fort overrun.
