Who We Are

Started Small.
Built on Trust.

It started in 2019 when two friends from Mahabaleshwar got tired of watching tourists overpay for unreliable rentals or struggle to find transport to reach Venna Lake or Pratapgad Fort on their own schedule.

Rajan and Suresh decided to start small — two bikes, a WhatsApp number, and a promise: honest prices, clean vehicles, no unnecessary hassle.

Today we have 6 vehicles, hundreds of happy riders, and the same promise we started with. Mahabaleshwar is our home — we want every visitor to experience it the right way.
