Required Documents

What to Bring

Bring the right documents to keep pickup quick and simple.

Bikes & Scooters

Valid 2-wheeler driving license (original only)
Aadhar Card OR Passport (original)
Minimum age: 18 years
Security Deposit: ₹1,000 — fully refundable

Cars & SUVs

Valid 4-wheeler driving license (original only)
Aadhar Card + one more ID (Passport / Voter ID)
Minimum age: 21 years
Security Deposit: ₹3,000 — fully refundable

International Tourists

Valid Passport (original)
International Driving Permit (IDP)
Contact us in advance to confirm
Security Deposit: ₹2,000 — fully refundable
We keep your documents safely during the rental period and return them when you bring the vehicle back.
